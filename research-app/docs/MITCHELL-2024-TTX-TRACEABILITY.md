## Study variants, not real designations

Table 1 (p. 16) compares eleven **notional** CCA-1 through CCA-11 configurations; the SEAD team also created a notional CCA-12 by modifying CCA-8 (p. 20). These numbered exercise variants must not be represented as fielded aircraft or silently equated to YFQ-42A / YFQ-44A. Use generic schematic silhouettes, not an invented type-specific technical drawing.

## Limits on paths, symbols, and timing

The report publishes concept graphics and descriptions, **not geospatial flight-track datasets**, true locations or precise waypoints, complete flight schedules, engagement telemetry, or mission outcomes. Accordingly, NexGen actors' coordinates, lines, motion keyframes, offsets and playback seconds remain **editorial**; they must not be called extracted flight paths. Map symbols indicate approximate class/function, not exact technical aircraft geometry. Figure 9's 24-hour SEAD cycle must not be interpreted as the application's 75-second playback duration. The paper's force-mix quantities are tabletop assumptions and requests, not real sorties flown.

No figures from the copyrighted report are duplicated in project assets. Cite the report by title, figure, and page when referencing its claims; preserve distinction among **documented report claim**, **TTX hypothetical concept**, and **NexGen illustrative rendering**.
