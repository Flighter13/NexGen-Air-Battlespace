# Mitchell Institute 2024 CCA tabletop exercise — source mapping

The four NexGen scenarios are research visualizations of concepts published in Mark A. Gunzinger, *Logistics While Under Attack: Key to a CCA Force Design* (Mitchell Institute for Aerospace Studies, February 2025). The report summarizes a **2024 tabletop exercise**, not actual flights, combat results, or instrumented flight tracks. The original PDF is cited by `mitchell-report` in the catalog and is not reproduced here.

The TTX CCA-1 through CCA-11 are **hypothetical force-design variants** listed in Table 1 (p. 16), *not* historical aircraft or equivalent to the named YFQ-42A / YFQ-44A prototypes. The SEAD team also created a notional CCA-12 by modifying CCA-8 (p. 20). Their catalog icons are schematic category symbols, not claimed physical configurations.

The diagram on p. 17 is explicitly labeled not necessarily representative of actual employment tactics. Do not extrapolate precise operational routes or tactical intercept geometries from the small-scale figures.

## Quantitative context

- The baseline maritime strike example shows **8 stand-off bombers, 8 penetrating bombers, 16 F-35A and 16 F-15EX** in each of two illustrated packages, with an exercise assumption of **two major strike pulses per day** during the opening phase (Fig. 3, p. 17). This is a **notional planning baseline**, not observed sorties.
- The teams' aggregate requests were **145%** of the exercise's notional available expendable CCA inventory (Fig. 11, p. 23).
- The logistics-constrained weapon-launch shares shifted from **96% to 68%** for the sweep team and **76% to 33%** for the SEAD team (Fig. 12, p. 25); these numbers describe exercise plans, not field measurements.
- The SEAD team requested **2,000** modified CCA-12 at a rough estimate of **$50,000 each** (p. 20); neither is an acquisition commitment.

## Artwork and attribution

All plotted animation paths are **editorial illustration**. Original NexGen schematic icon assets depict representative platform families only. Existing project photographs retain their own attribution statements; report figures are referenced, not copied.

## Known visual caveats

The HVAA map currently uses a generic support symbol rather than misrepresenting E-3 Sentry as E-7A Wedgetail. Existing scene geometries are maintained until separately designed and tested as clearly labeled schematic depictions. Future improvements can add dedicated E-7A, F-15EX, KJ-500 and notional CCA role symbols, and display numerical TTX data directly in the app without implying live telemetry.
