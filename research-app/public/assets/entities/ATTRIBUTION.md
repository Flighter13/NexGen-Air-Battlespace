# Entity images

`f35.png`, `yfq-42.png`, and `yfq-44.png` are unchanged copies of the user's existing NexGen-Air-Battlespace repository images, retrieved 2026-09-17. Their original upstream creators and licenses were not recorded in the legacy repository. No public-domain or ownership claim is made. Their file-level GitHub origins are recorded in the entity JSON and displayed in the application.

`fighter.svg`, `uncrewed.svg`, and `support.svg` are original abstract vector symbols created for this application. They are presentation artwork, not depictions of a particular platform, engineering drawings, or evidence of capabilities. These symbols are used in the geographic/network views to keep markers consistent and avoid treating a notional research entity as a named production aircraft.
